# Motor Policy Exclusions

## Clause 6.1 Wear and Tear

The policy does not cover damage arising from normal wear and tear,
gradual deterioration, or mechanical or electrical breakdown.

## Clause 6.3 Unlisted Drivers

The policy does not cover loss or damage occurring while the vehicle is
being driven by a person who is not listed as an authorized driver on the
policy schedule.

## Clause 6.5 Racing and Off-Road Use

The policy does not cover loss or damage occurring while the vehicle is
being used for racing, rallying, speed testing, or off-road driving.
