# Motor Coverage Guide

## Clause 3.1 Accidental Damage

The policy covers accidental damage to the insured vehicle caused by
collision, fire, or attempted theft, subject to the excess shown on the
policy schedule.

## Clause 3.4 Third-Party Liability

The policy covers legal liability for injury to third parties and damage
to third-party property arising from an accident involving the insured
vehicle, up to the limit stated in the policy schedule.

## Clause 4.2 Windscreen Cover

Accidental damage to the front, rear, or side windscreen is covered when
windscreen cover is included in the policy schedule. The standard excess
applies unless the repair is completed through an approved repair partner.
