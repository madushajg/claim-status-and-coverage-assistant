# Claims Process FAQs

## How long does a claim assessment take?

Claim assessment timelines depend on the type of claim, the documents
received, and the complexity of the assessment.

Customers should refer to the latest claim timeline for the current
progress of an individual claim.

## What documents are required to support a claim?

Customers are typically asked to provide a description of the incident,
photographs of the damage, and any relevant third-party or police
reports.

## How is the claim outcome communicated?

Customers are notified of the claim outcome through the claims
timeline and a direct communication from the assigned claims team once
the assessment is complete.
