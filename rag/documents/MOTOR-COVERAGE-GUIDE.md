# Motor Coverage Guide

## Windscreen Damage

Windscreen cover may include accidental damage to the front, rear, or side
windscreen when the benefit is included in the policy schedule.

Customers should check their policy schedule to confirm whether the benefit
is included.

The applicable excess and repair-partner conditions should also be checked.

## Understanding Coverage

A coverage guide provides a plain-language explanation of policy benefits.
The policy wording and policy schedule remain the authoritative sources
for determining coverage.
