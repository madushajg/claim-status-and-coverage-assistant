# Claims Process FAQs

## What happens after a claim is reported?

A claim may progress through several stages, including registration,
document review, assessment, and settlement or closure.

The current status and timeline of an individual claim must be retrieved
from the Claims API.

## How can I check what has happened to my claim?

The Claims API provides the current claim status and timeline events.

The assistant should use live claim data rather than infering claim progress
from general process documentation.

## Does the FAQ determine whether a claim is covered?

No. Coverage decisions must be grounded in the applicable policy wording
and policy schedule.
