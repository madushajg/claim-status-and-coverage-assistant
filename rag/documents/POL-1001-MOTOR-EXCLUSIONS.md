# Motor Policy Exclusions

## Clause 7.1 General Exclusions

Coverage is subject to the exclusions stated in the policy.

An exclusion must not be inferred from the absence of a coverage statement.
The relevant policy wording and applicable conditions must be reviewed.

## Clause 7.2 Coverage Limits

A coverage benefit may be subject to limits, conditions, or an excess.

The applicable policy schedule and policy wording determine the conditions
for the relevant benefit.
